Order Progression

What Happens Once You Submit.

A clear roadmap of how your brick inquiry moves from quantity takeoff to on-site delivery.

1

Technical Takeoff

Within 4 business hours, our brick engineer reviews your dimensions or architectural drawings to calculate exact square meterage and pallet count.

2

Factory Quote & Test Certificates

We provide a formal proforma invoice with locked factory pallet rates, delivery haulage, and compressive strength test data.

3

Batch Allocation & Packaging

Your bricks are strapped, palletized, and shrink-wrapped at our Owerri yard to guarantee zero chipped edges during transit.

4

Site Dispatch & Masonry Handover

Direct flatbed or tipper delivery to site foundation, with our certified bricklaying crew mobilizing for structural execution if requested.
