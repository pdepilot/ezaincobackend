Architectural Masonry Capabilities

Engineered Brick Supply & Masonry.

From direct factory batch production to master structural bricklaying and high-end exterior exposed facades, E-zainco Brick delivers durable, climate-resilient brickwork engineered to outlast generations.

Core Service 01

Bulk Brick Supply & Manufacturing

Direct factory supply of high-density Compressed Earth Bricks (CEB), interlocking blocks, and kiln-fired red clay facing bricks. We manufacture to exact dimensional tolerances with certified compressive strengths.

Key Specifications & Deliverables

  • Hydraulic compression rated at 8.5 – 12.0 MPa load capacity
  • Palletized, strapped, and shrink-wrapped for zero transport chipping
  • Natural mineral pigments: Terracotta Ochre, Red Clay, and Charcoal
  • Nationwide flatbed and tipper logistics direct to site foundation

Core Service 02

Architectural Masonry & Structural Bricklaying

Full structural brick construction executed by our certified master bricklayers. From load-bearing duplexes to civic edifices, we ensure millimeter-precise coursing, uniform mortar joints, and lifetime stability.

Key Specifications & Deliverables

  • English, Flemish, Stretcher, and Stack bond bricklaying patterns
  • Anti-efflorescence, high-tensile polymer-modified mortar bonding
  • Integrated damp-proof courses (DPC) and reinforced lintel arches
  • Laser-verified plumb verticality and horizontal bed joint alignment

Core Service 03

Exposed Brick Cladding & Facades

Upgrade existing concrete or blockwork walls with premium exterior brick veneers and terracotta cladding. Achieves an authentic exposed brick aesthetic without structural rebuilds.

Key Specifications & Deliverables

  • Thin-brick veneer slips (20–30mm) for internal and external masonry walls
  • Heavy-duty stainless mechanical anchors and polymer adhesion
  • Silane-siloxane invisible hydrophobic weatherproofing impregnation
  • Zero maintenance finish: Never requires painting, peeling, or re-plastering

Manufacturing Rigor

E-zainco Brick Production Standards.

We eliminate fragile sandcrete blocks. Every brick unit produced in our facility is subjected to strict hydraulic pressure and dimensional calibration.

High Compressive Strength

While typical sandcrete blocks crumble at 2.5 MPa, our hydraulic compressed earth and fired bricks reach 8.5 to 15.0 MPa, easily bearing multi-story structural loads.

Water & Efflorescence Barrier

Formulated with mineral stabilizers and treated with silane repellents to block tropical rainfall absorption, preventing white salt efflorescence and dampness.

Thermal Comfort & Fire Rating

High thermal mass naturally cools interior temperatures during hot Nigerian afternoons, reducing AC power consumption, while offering a 4-hour non-combustible fire rating.
